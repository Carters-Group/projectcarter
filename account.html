Portfolio plan

One blended growth rate applied across the whole portfolio rather than property-by-property, and debt is assumed to reduce the same way Portfolio Review models it - 50% of surplus cash after interest goes to principal, at the blended rate implied by your saved properties. No capital gains tax is included here - whether and when to sell is your call, not this plan's.
