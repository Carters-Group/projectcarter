Your portfolio

Cash flow before tax

Rent from your current leases, less the running costs and loan interest you have entered. Tax on that income is not included.

If you sold everything

Each property sold on its own sale date and price (today and current value unless you set them), less selling costs, the loan balance and capital gains tax under current law. The full workings are under Tax and land tax below.

Tax and land tax

Land tax by state and capital gains tax if you sold, built from the tax details on each property below. Estimates only, not tax advice.

Debt reduction goal

A simple planning figure that is not saved anywhere. Assumes your debt is interest-only, so every dollar contributed goes straight to principal, at the blended rate across your properties.

Portfolio plan

One blended growth rate applied across the whole portfolio rather than property by property. Debt is assumed to reduce the way the ROI calculator models it: 50% of surplus cash after interest goes to principal, at your blended rate. No capital gains tax is included, since nothing here assumes you are selling.

How your cash flow would hold up under three stress scenarios, applied across your whole portfolio at once.

Approximate: uses your portfolio's blended rate rather than each property's own terms. Rate rise and vacancy are shown as separate scenarios, not stacked together, since both hitting at once in the same year is unlikely. A guide to overall cash-flow exposure, not a precise forecast.
