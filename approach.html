How I Work / Australia-Wide

My Approach

Find the problem site. Fix what's broken. Build something with character.

Artist's impression, indicative only

Every site I take on runs the same way: find what makes the numbers, the approval and the partners all work at once. That's true whether it's a development site with a planning problem, a commercial building trading below its potential or a stalled project that needs a new way in. I work Australia-wide, and I stay on the job from first look to sale or hold.

The process

Five stages, and a site can come in at any one of them:

  • Finding the way in. Working out whether a site or building actually stacks up: cost to buy, cost to deliver, and what it's worth at the other end.
  • Planning pathways. Reading a site's real planning risk, not the optimistic version: what a council will actually approve, what consultant reports it will actually need, and what a pre-lodgement meeting will surface before you've spent the money finding out the hard way.
  • Design with character. Deciding what the place should feel like before it gets value-engineered into another box: facades, proportions, materials, gardens and the details people remember.
  • Funding & joint ventures. Getting a project funded properly with the right mix of equity, debt and partners, on terms where the incentives line up.
  • Delivery. From construction through to sale or lease-up, keeping the project on the numbers it was approved on.

What I take on

Development. Residential and mixed-use projects from raw site to handover. I've taken projects the full distance, sold out to owner-occupiers, and Royal Terraces is where that's heading next.

Value-add commercial. Lifting under-performing buildings with re-leasing, refurbishment, planning uplift and new income. This is live work, not a theory: it's what's underway on 189 Balaclava Road right now.

Problem sites. The roadblock cases: planning refusals, difficult titles, stalled builds, partner disputes. Finding the way in that everyone else has missed is the actual job, not an exception to it.

Character over boxes. I'd rather build something a street is glad to have. Heritage-inspired design, painted facades, real gardens and homes that still perform on energy.

Joint ventures. If you own a site you can't get moving, I can come in as co-developer, with a structure built so everyone's incentives actually line up.

Private capital. Family offices and private investors looking for a development partner on specific projects, with aligned terms and plain reporting.

Run your own numbers

I've built seven free calculators so you can stress-test a deal before we ever speak, using the same kind of modelling I run on my own projects:

  • Development feasibility (DA) cost calculator: cost from buying a site to a lodged DA
  • Development feasibility (GRV) calculator: full feasibility from DA to settled sales
  • Net operating income (NOI) calculator: income, outgoings and implied value on a commercial asset
  • Property ROI calculator: return on a property hold from purchase to sale
  • Portfolio review calculator: after-tax cash flow across a multi-property portfolio
  • Commercial lending calculator: deposit and equity position by loan type
  • Tax depreciation calculator: a year-by-year deduction estimate for the building and its fittings

Figures carry across them as a deal progresses: a valuation from the NOI calculator can feed straight into the ROI or lending numbers, and sale proceeds from a completed deal can fund the next one. See the full calculator suite.

If you hold property through a self-managed super fund, the Commercial Lending calculator covers SMSF purchases and your account works out capital gains at the fund's rate. It's general information to help you see where you stand, before you take specialist SMSF advice.
