Royal Terraces

6 Royal Street, Toowoomba City, Queensland

Visit royalterraces.com.au
Artist's impression, indicative only

Royal Terraces is nine heritage-inspired homes on the corner of Royal and Snell Streets in Toowoomba City: eight two-bedroom terraces and one courtyard home, a short walk from the heart of town.

It's the project that best sums up what I'm trying to do. An inner-city corner that could easily have taken another row of modern boxes gets painted facades, iron-lace balconies, timber friezes and a garden at the gate instead. The aim is a terrace street that feels like it was always here.

Design & energy

I set the design direction and worked it up in collaboration with Van Heerden Design Studio. Character doesn't have to cost performance: the homes have a 7.9 star average energy rating (average of the nine homes, 7.1 to 8.4 stars), with rooftop solar included.

Inside, every home gets the same Sage interior: herringbone floors, sage panelled cabinetry, a brick-look feature wall and brushed brass. Each parapet carries a plaque, and buyers can choose the name on theirs.

The street

Your name on the parapet

Terraces used to carry a name. These do again. Each home has a plaque at the top of its facade, and the buyer can choose what it says, subject to the developer's approval under the contract.

It's a small thing, and it's exactly the sort of detail that disappears when every building is value-engineered into the same box.
