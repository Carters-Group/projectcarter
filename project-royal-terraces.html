Royal Terraces

6 Royal Street, Toowoomba City, Queensland

Royal Terraces is a limited release of ten Art Deco-inspired terrace homes on an inner-city corner in Toowoomba City. It's a Project Carter development, currently working through the planning stages ahead of a staged release.

The design leans into a modern-industrial character — exposed textures, high ceilings and clever spatial planning that makes roughly 100m² of internal living feel considerably larger. Each terrace reads as its own home within a single, deliberately composed street frontage, finished with a professionally commissioned mural.

Design & sustainability

The homes target an average 8-star NatHERS rating with rooftop solar integrated from the outset, pairing timeless architectural detailing with contemporary, low-running-cost performance. The intent is a home that is bold yet genuinely liveable — suited to creative owner-occupiers who want something with more personality than a standard townhouse.

Private car parking and exterior garden boxing are built into each dwelling, with the inner-city position putting the city centre, rail and parkland within an easy walk.
