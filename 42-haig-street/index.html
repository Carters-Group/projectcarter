Haig On The Park

Less house to look after, more comfort while you're in it

Haig On The Park is a small group of four homes on an inner-city block in South Toowoomba, backing onto Gold Park, with the shops at the end of the street. Each is on one level, with open-plan living, dining and kitchen opening to a covered alfresco and a private fenced courtyard.

Built for owner-occupiers and downsizers who want a new, low-maintenance home close to town.

Behind a gatehouse entry there are shared gardens with two communal vegetable plots and compost hubs. Every home has two car spaces, one under its own carport, plus a visitor space on site.

Energy efficiency

7.8 stars, with solar on the roof

Unit 2 is rated 7.8 stars under NatHERS, the national energy rating scheme for homes, the highest in the group alongside Unit 4. More stars means less heating and cooling to stay comfortable, so lower power bills through a Toowoomba winter and summer. Each home also has its own rooftop solar system.

The living, dining and alfresco face north, for winter sun and cross-breezes in summer.

Location

Gold Park behind you, the shops at the end of the street

The homes back straight onto Gold Park. Walk four minutes to the end of Haig Street and you're at the Ruthven Street corner, with takeaway, ice cream, a café and a convenience store. Aldi and Coles are about fifteen minutes on foot, and the hospital and CBD are a short drive.
