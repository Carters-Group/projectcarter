Project Carter, Corporate Property Advisor

A creative advisor to property developers, value-add commercial, family offices and managed funds.

I'm Trent Macartney. I advise developers, private families and funds on property that does not fit a template: sites with a planning problem, commercial assets trading below their potential, and deals that have stalled.

My work runs the full cycle. Feasibility and site strategy, planning pathways, capital structuring and joint ventures, and delivery oversight from acquisition through to sale or lease-up. I have taken residential projects from raw site to sold out, and repositioned commercial assets to lift income, tenure and value.

Where most people see a roadblock, I look for another way in. There is always a solution to every property. The work is finding the one that makes the numbers, the approval and the partners all work at once.
