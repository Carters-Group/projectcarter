Project Carter — Property Development

Boutique townhouse communities and commercial assets, developed to add lasting value.

Project Carter is an Australian development business focused on two things we do well: delivering well-designed townhouse projects that people want to live in, and acquiring under-performing commercial property we can reposition and improve.

We work across the full development cycle — site acquisition, feasibility, planning approvals, design management and construction delivery — with a small, hands-on team and trusted consultant and builder partners.

Our projects are located Australia-wide. If you have a site, a mandate or capital looking for the right partner, we would like to hear from you.
